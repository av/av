This is a history of how I build software with AI, told as a single diagram. It begins in 2022 with one laptop, one editor and a browser tab, and ends in September 2026 with a phone, a devbox and a small fleet of machines whose agents report to a controller.

Scroll to move through it. Each step adds, removes or moves a piece of the setup and explains why; what changed is lit and everything else recedes. Green marks anything agentic, so you can watch it spread. The bar at the bottom of the diagram jumps to any step.

Most write-ups of an agent setup show only the final picture, which is the least interesting part. What matters is the order in which the pieces arrived and what each one fixed.
